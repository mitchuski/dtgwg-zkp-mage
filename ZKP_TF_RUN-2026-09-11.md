geoffturk (09-10, #9 point 4) is right: a VDC/VAC chain check is *hidden-value equality* — a child's `issuer` MUST
equal its parent's `credentialSubject.id`, across credentials signed by different parties, with **no holder secret
involved** — not "two identifiers open to one secret". Record 007's `request` row and its provenance carry the
"four things lean on the unencoded common-control linkage" line from #31. Proposed revision (state unchanged):

- `request`/provenance: "two of the four #42 predicates (the community-anchored proof; the VMC+VAC shared-subject
  rule) rest on this record; the two chain predicates are hidden-value equality (record 020 clause 1 / the VAC record)".
- Statement/issuance: widen to identifiers a controller holds **as subject or issuer** (the VRC-issuer case is
  statement 3 of 010).

  its VMC-side identifier") and the candidate requirement sentence once #9 lands it.

  geoffturk wants carried — quote it back on #9.

### 3.3 New record candidate · 021 VAC attenuation chain

The credential spec added a predicate with "no stated primitive" (PR #42): *holder holds a VAC conferring action X at
scope S; the chain is valid and unrevoked: each link issued by its parent's subject, narrows its parent, no link revoked,
depth within every `maxAttenuation` and ≤ 8, root issued by the party governing S — without disclosing the chain.* Plus:
the VAC is not a bearer credential (key control at invocation), and when membership and authority are proven together
with the subject withheld, the presentation MUST include a shared-subject proof. Composes: chain-resolve ∧
signature-verify ∧ key-binding (004) ∧ non-revocation (006) ∧ transcript-bind (003) ∧ [009 hidden-equality for
issuer = parent-subject] ∧ [007/012 for the VMC shared-subject]. Request: cred-spec PR #42 editor's note + §Attenuation.
Differences from 020 worth a fresh disclosure set: attenuation is *by default* (opposite of VDC), the subject acts as
itself, revocation cascades, and the governing party may require the leaf subject to independently qualify (a VMC).

### 3.4 New primitive candidate · 009 hidden-value equality across credentials

The gadget both chain records and the shared-subject rule need and none of the eight primitives binds: *two committed
values, in two credentials signed by different parties, are equal* — with no holder secret in the relation. It is
the thing geoffturk asked us to "name separately so it does not appear covered". One primitive record, one gadget
(`commitment-open` twice + equality, or a new `hidden-equality` entry in the closed gadget list), fixture family
`rejects-unsat: values-differ`. Records 020, 021 and the VMC+VAC shared-subject case then compose it explicitly.
