## Found for WG-16: a self-vouch is counted

Record 023 has **no "voucher is not the applicant" clause**. Its negative space allows the applicant to be a member
already. The two together let the applicant vouch for itself through its own grant.

`self_vouch_counted` builds the case concretely:
- the relation accepts k = 2;
- the statement holds, reached through `soundness` with every hash hypothesis discharged;
- one voucher's grant identifier is under the applicant's own secret.

Record 024 excludes this as clause 7 ("no attester is the applicant").

Historical alternatives considered before the negative-space fold (not pending alternatives):
- **either** add a clause 8 to 023: no voucher's grant is under the applicant's secret. It is a `distinctness` or
  `key-binding` clause: the applicant proves its admission identifier's secret differs from each voucher's.
- **or** add a `doesNotEstablish` line: *that no voucher is the applicant — an applicant who is already a member can
  vouch for itself through its own grant*, next to line 2.
