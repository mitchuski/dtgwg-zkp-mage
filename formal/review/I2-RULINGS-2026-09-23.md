## Findings requiring model or claim changes

1. **013: committed bytes are not bound to the policy form or member leaf.** Accepts takes bytes, form and subjectLeaf independently. soundness proves opening uniqueness, form membership and conditional subjectLeaf membership separately; it cannot establish that the committed half has that form or subject. review/R013Binding.lean checks two concrete witnesses under injective, domain-separated toy hashes: a committed member=true form passes as member=false; alternatively the proof uses an unrelated member leaf. Both witnesses pass Lean without added axioms. This is a model/spec binding gap, not a cryptographic attack on an implementation.
2. **013: exchange answers are unauthenticated Boolean events.** exchange_safe proves state ordering; it does not derive answers from verified direction proofs, bind proofs to cA/cB, check a challenge, or validate revealed bytes. Clause 4 is outside the model. no_fairness remains a valid and useful negative result.
3. **010: supplied leaves, VRC endpoints and identifiers lack extraction bindings.** Voucher-side common control is missing. The handle list may be empty and is not required to contain the three credential handles. The theorem proves its conjunction, but metadata saying it establishes a relationship between these two current members is stronger than the encoded relation.
4. **023: clause 5 contains a key requirement absent from the model.** subj=adm or CommonControl supplies an identifier relation, not a presentation key derivation. Membership, member distinctness and handle non-revocation proofs remain useful under their field-authentication assumptions. The self-vouch result and admission boundary remain intact.
5. **021: parent digest absent.** LinkedConstraint proves issuer/parent-subject equality only. VHop has no authority.parent digest. The same-identifier clause-7 membership route is connected by hLeafSubj; a different-identifier common-control route is not modelled. Ceiling 8 bounds links below the root; confirm the record uses this convention.
6. **024: useful counting evidence with explicit scope.** Extractable and stable secrets support distinct Member counting and member-level exclusion. No identifier-from-applicant-secret relation establishes clause 5. The spent set is sequential and detached from the counting rule, profile/interval encoding and a durable transaction. It does not prove aggregate issuance accounting, retries or per-vetter attribution. H-applicant can hold vacuously; no natural-person exclusion is inferred.

007's refined derivation qualification fits the opening model; its privacy claims still need separate arguments. 011's presenter-side key composition and 012's star-to-clique result fit their narrow scope. 020/021 propagation proofs fit the supplied fields; signed field extraction/root authority remain premises.
