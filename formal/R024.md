## What is proved, and what it rests on

The construction's cryptography is abstract. What the construction is claimed to guarantee enters as **named
hypotheses**, never axioms. Each theorem proves: *if* the construction has these properties, the community's
counting rule gives the record's statement. When the source is public, each hypothesis becomes one concrete
check to run against it.

| hypothesis | record source | what it will demand of the source |
|---|---|---|
| **H-Σ** `Extractable` | clauses 1, 2, 4 | knowledge soundness of the Σ-PS proof: an accepted proof yields, per tag, a live-label class credential whose secret is not the applicant's |
| **H-stable** `SecretStable` | issuance 3 (added from this model) | a vetter keeps its secret identifier across label rotations — or the rule forbids one vetter holding credentials under two labels in one proof |
| **H-applicant** `ApplicantSecret` | clause 4 | if the applicant holds any class credential, it is over the applicant's own secret (vacuous for a first-time joiner) |
| **H-fresh** `SecretFresh` | issuance 4 (blind issuance) | two members never hold one secret under one label *(completeness only)* |
| **H-tag** `TagInjective` | clause 2 | Tag_DDH has no collisions for a fixed applicant *(completeness only)* |
| one credential per member per label | issuance 4 | structural in the model (`cred` is a function); in the source, the community's store check before blind-signing |

## The negative space (the record's `doesNotEstablish` list)

| line | classification | where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · k attesters are k distinct people | **disproved** without enrolment (one person, two memberships: rule accepts, statement holds, no two distinct people) · **proved** given one membership per person | `distinct_members_not_distinct_people`, `people_of_members` |
| 2 · the holder is the member it was issued to | out of scope: the model identifies a credential with its member; H-Σ extracts a secret, not a holder, so a handed-over credential is outside what any counting theorem can see | — |
| 3 · vetter role current inside the period | **disproved**: a withdrawn vetter's live class credential still counts; no proof-side hypothesis closes it (record's answer: rotation, or 023 clause 6) | `live_label_not_current_role` |
| 4 · session happened as attested | out of scope: the vetter's check of the person is outside the proof | — |
| 5 · anything to a third party | out of scope: the model has one verifier, the issuing community | — |
| 6 · the applicant is admitted | out of scope: verifying, counting and issuing are three acts (WG-04) | — |
| 7 · any applicant–vetter relationship | out of scope: no VRC in the model | — |

## Found while proving: a re-keyed vetter is counted twice

`rekey_counts_twice`: the record lets one proof mix attestations from either side of a rotation. If a vetter's
secret identifier changes at rotation, its two tags are distinct. The rule then accepts k = 2 from **one** vetter,
and H-stable fails. So the soundness proof depends on H-stable. It is a real property the construction has to
have, not an assumption made for convenience. **Folded 2026-09-23 (G3)** as record 024 **issuance requirement 3**: a vetter's
class credentials under successive labels are over one secret identifier, or the construction refuses a proof spanning
two vetter labels. **Question for the constructor:** which holds? No rejection code: the community cannot detect a
re-key, so the requirement sits on issuance.

## Not modelled

Per-vetter token caps (the constructor lists the cap as per-group; colluding vetters can share tokens). Unlinkability
and the adversary claims (these need a computational model, not this counting model). Proof sizes (constructor-reported,
`measured: false`).
