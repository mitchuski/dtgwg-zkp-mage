## 1. The practice returned to its purpose

The turn began with a connection already present in the work. A practice course for circuit optimization had exercised the discipline of proposing, proving, measuring and independently checking a bounded change. Mitch named the deeper route: the same discipline belongs beside the DTG ZKP specification, where communities need proofs suited to graph presentations and credentials.

The point was weaving. OpenVTC conversations, community onboarding, the construction cards, prior verifier runs, the ZK Book and the privacy architecture already supplied the threads. The next useful artifact was a bench on which those requests could become reproducible examples.

Mitch made the placement precise: a new directory inside the DTG evidence repository, expanding the practice of running circuits on your own box. The unpublished practice project could contribute a method, but could not serve as a public reference or required dependency. The method therefore had to be written out in the new directory, with public pins and enough instructions for someone arriving without this conversation.

An initial route through record010 would have demonstrated a member showing a relationship with an offline voucher. ## 3. The guide, specification and executable evidence keep their jobs

The earlier decision to separate teaching from the core specification remains intact. The core carries construction definitions and conformance. The companion guide explains. Lean/Clean supplies a candidate formalization with stated assumptions and checkable obligations. The evidence bench records what actually ran.

A source proof is valuable without being everything. It does not certify a serializer, exporter, witness runtime, proving backend or admission decision by implication. Nor does an inverse-based inequality check authenticate the two values it compares. Those gaps are part of the contract, not footnotes to be forgotten after a good score.

## 4. Two seats, one bounded machine

The constructor built baseline and optimized Clean assertions for distinctness and equality. The assayer's job is to read the frozen contract, check the exact source, rebuild the theorems, inspect their axioms and preserve counterexamples. The accompanying results and receipt state the actual completion level; this narrative does not substitute for them.

The baseline materializes an intermediate difference; the candidate keeps that affine expression inline. Distinctness retains an inverse witness and its nonzero constraint. Equality retains the equality constraint. These are deliberately small arithmetic examples, suitable for teaching and inspection, not claims of a frontier record or complete admission circuit.

The old verifier bench remains useful: canonical tests passed11/11; the Python consumer passed6/6 and consumed39/39 existing vectors. The fixtures suite's regeneration attempt stopped on filesystem permissions and is not recorded as passed. That distinction belongs in the handoff as much as a successful theorem.

One heavy build at a time, shared existing dependencies and small retained artifacts keep the current machine usable. Hardware sponsorship should follow measured demand. A second model context on this machine is not a second participant's independent-hardware reproduction.

## 5. The City receives a workshop, not a new authority

The course is a place for mages to practice stating and improving a spell. The workshop lets that practice answer a community's actual request. A chronicle preserves why; a tome explains; the guide teaches; the construction record states; the evidence tests.

The agentprivacy contribution is the separation between proposal and checking, careful accounting of disclosure, privacy-value questions and the memory needed to resume responsibly. These habits do not confer a cryptographic privacy theorem. The future agentic compiler remains a proposal for assembling and reviewing supported predicates under explicit profiles, not an automatic issuer or a finished trust-graph service.

## 6. The location correction and the next pair

Mitch clarified that the practice project should live directly under the user root. It was moved there without copying its dependency cache. Eleven internal junctions were retargeted, the resume bytes were checked across the move, and the existing harness runtime was made reachable through a sibling junction. Historical logs keep their original paths. This was a filesystem relocation, not creation or publication of a standalone Git repository.

The DTG contribution remains independent of that project. Its new directory carries a proposal, source register, contract, dual-harness protocol, runnable package, results and a resume record. The next pair should begin with those files, not infer completion from this story. Full023 integration, backend verification, external reproduction and human publication are later evidence steps.
