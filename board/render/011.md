# CARD 011 · Pairwise edge (VRC possession, directed personas shown, pairwise identifiers hidden)

**kind:** composed · **state:** `carded` · **priority:** P2 · **owner:** mitchuski · **composes:** 003 ∧ 004 ∧ 006 ∧ 007

## Dish
A verifier learns that two disclosed persona identifiers (declared `directed`) hold a valid relationship credential between them, without learning the pairwise-scope identifiers under it and without a handle that correlates this presentation with any other.

## Ingredients (witness — never leaves the holder)
- the VRC and the pairwise-scope identifier pair it was issued between
- the co-control witnesses linking each disclosed `directed` persona identifier to its hidden pairwise identifier (card 007; cred-spec #9: co-control proven in ZK, never a field) — the counterparty’s half is theirs to supply
- the presenter’s holder secret

## Pantry (public inputs)
- the two `directed` persona identifiers (disclosed on purpose)
- rl_root, epoch
- transcriptDigest

## Method
1. the VRC verifies under the issuing pairwise identifier’s key → `signature-verify`
2. each disclosed persona identifier is co-controlled with its hidden pairwise identifier (card 007) — the presenter’s from their own secret, the counterparty’s from the counterparty’s attestation → `key-binding` (card 007)
3. the presenter’s presentation key derives from the secret behind their pairwise identifier → `key-binding` (card 004)
4. the VRC handle is not revoked at epoch → `non-revocation` (card 006)
5. bound to one transcript → `transcript-bind` (card 003)

## Yield (the disclosure set)
- the two `directed` persona identifiers
- rl_root, epoch
- transcriptDigest

## Does not establish
- any community-level assurance (that is card 010)
- that the personas are distinct natural persons
- the relationship's content beyond what the statement discloses
- the counterparty’s persona↔pairwise linkage without the counterparty’s attestation (card 007 negative space)

## Adversary
- pairwise identifiers hidden; no cross-presentation correlator minted by the linkage itself — against: verifier, verifiers-colluding

## Horizon
- VRC validity
- status freshness

## Tasting
families: accepts · rejects-verify · unlinkable
rejection codes: `co-control-unproven`, `vrc-signature-invalid`
