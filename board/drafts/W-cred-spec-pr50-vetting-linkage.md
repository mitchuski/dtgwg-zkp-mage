The distinction between recording a vetting procedure and endorsing a person helps the ZKP mapping. The profile's PASS limits should carry into any private presentation: an authenticated vetting statement does not establish admission, current membership or identity truth. For the community-anchored construction, `identityCommitment` also needs to remain distinct from the voucher-to-membership linkage; committing to identity claims does not supply that relation (record 010's issuance line; record 008's first route is the shape of the commitment itself). One thing sharpened since this was drafted: under trust-tasks-tf #531 (merged 21 September) a vetting attestation travels in the presentation and is replaced with it, so whatever the card digest covers is what a consumer recomputes from the bytes it was handed. Two asks: make the distinction explicit in the example, and state whether the digest covers the received bytes or the JCS canonical form before implementers bind it into a proof.
